3-Step Local Development Workflow

        
Spin up a local devnet, test Clarity contracts, and wire your frontend UI in minutes.

      


      

        

          Step 01
          
Scaffold with Clarinet

          
Run clarinet new my-btc-app to generate a local contract test suite and deterministic devnet chain.

        

        

          Step 02
          
Verify Static Safety

          
Execute clarinet check to catch type mismatches, unchecked errors, or asset leaks prior to testnet deploy.

        

        

          Step 03
          
Connect Frontend

          
Use @stacks/connect in React to authenticate Xverse or Leather wallets and broadcast contract calls.
