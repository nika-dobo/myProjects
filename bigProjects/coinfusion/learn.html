Core Architecture

Stacks settles transactions on Bitcoin without modifying Bitcoin's base protocol.

01 · Consensus

Proof of Transfer (PoX)

Miners commit BTC to produce Stacks blocks, and those BTC rewards flow directly to STX holders who lock tokens in consensus cycles.

02 · Security

Bitcoin Settlement

Every Stacks block anchors its cryptographic hash to a Bitcoin transaction, inheriting Bitcoin's hashpower finality.

03 · Yield

Earn Native BTC

Instead of inflationary token emissions, Stackers receive real Bitcoin payouts across ~2-week consensus reward cycles.
