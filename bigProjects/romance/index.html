🚀 Joint Venture

Between lines of code

Giorgi and Nini were two complete strangers. They met at one of the Georgian online hackathons, where everyone was randomly paired up. Giorgi was good at programming, and Nini had an amazing sense of design. At first, they focused only on the project — a registration page, an attractive homepage, and a little JavaScript. But as the nights passed, the dialogue between them became more than just technical. Nini once said cheerfully: “There’s love in your code,” and Giorgi replied: “You give your design soul.” They worked together all week — Zoom sessions, shared screens, randomly sent smileys, and music they listened to together in the background. The project was a success. They took second place in the hackathon, but for them, the real victory had already begun. When they finally met in person, Nini smiled quietly and said: “You know, you were my best project.” They still work together today – one writes code, the other draws the world. They say love comes when you least expect it – sometimes when you think you're just building a website.

Letters left in the past

Ani and Luka were childhood friends. They lived next door and left letters in each other’s mailboxes every day — drawings, heartfelt phrases, and even little secrets. Years passed, life moved on, and Ani moved to another city, while Luka stayed in the old neighborhood. One night, Ani decided to return to the house of the past. Someone new had moved in next door, but in her old mailbox she found a strange little envelope. Inside was a letter — written to Ani by Luka as a child, but never sent. “If you ever come back and find this, I want you to know that everything has changed since you left. Childhood ended for me with your eyes.” Ani couldn’t believe that she had found the letter years later. She reached out to Luka on social media and sent him a photo of the letter. The answer was not long in coming: “I didn’t have the courage to search for you back then. But now I hope we can write everything from scratch… like in a brand new letter.” Today they are writing that story together in real life — where childhood memories have blossomed into enduring love.

After the video call

Tamar first met Nika on Zoom for a school project. They had a strange first encounter — neither wanted to turn on their camera, but in the end they clicked the button at the exact same moment. At first, it was just teamwork. The project was about technology, but the conversations between them quickly drifted to everything else — plans for the future, favorite records, and childhood dreams. They started meeting late every evening via video call. Tamar says Nika's laughter was unforgettable. Nika remembers Tamar saying: “I never thought I could feel so close to someone through a screen.” The project ended and the work was submitted, but the calls never ceased. Often there was no need for words — they would simply leave the video running, looking up occasionally and listening to shared playlists. One evening, Nika held up a small handwritten card to his webcam that read: “Please come meet me in the real world.” Tamar smiled warmly and answered the call: “I’ve been waiting for that for so long.”

Just one comment

Elene never thought that a comment left on YouTube would change the course of her life. An old video of nostalgic romantic melodies moved her so deeply that she wrote: “This song touches a quiet corner of my soul.” Within hours, a reply appeared from a stranger named Data: “Me too. It felt like it walked into my heart with both sadness and warmth.” Their dialogue continued in the comments before moving to Instagram, where they swapped rare tracks. Conversations about music naturally grew into stories about their fears, families, and hopes. For months, they communicated only through text and voice notes. Data once wrote: “I prefer the melody of your voice to any symphony.” Weeks later, they arranged to meet. Data brought a small cassette tape containing an acoustic piece he had recorded for her, opening with the words of her original comment. Sometimes true love begins with a single comment — and becomes a melody that echoes for a lifetime.

Heart sent by mistake

On an ordinary Tuesday afternoon, Mariam received a Telegram message from an unknown number: “Good evening, how are you? ❤️” She replied politely: “I think you have the wrong number.” The stranger, Saba, replied with a laugh: “If our story started like this, maybe I shouldn’t rush to leave?” Mariam found his wit disarming. You wouldn't expect a conversation with a complete stranger to feel so comfortable, but it did. They continued talking the next day, and the next. Days turned into weeks. Once Saba wrote: “Sometimes I think that random emoji sent by mistake was the truest direction I ever took.” When they finally met face-to-face, nervous anticipation melted instantly. Saba placed a small card in Mariam’s hand: “I wasn’t mistaken about your number — and I definitely wasn’t mistaken about you.”

An app inspired by you

Levan had loved coding since childhood, but was quiet and reserved in real-world friendships. He noticed Nini at school — an artist always sketching in her notebook. One day, Nini gathered the courage to slide a small sketch across Levan's desk. It depicted their names beside a beautifully drawn application icon. Beneath it was written: “Code with love — with your mind and your heart.” Levan recognized it as an invitation to create something together. Over the next several months, they built “Nino” — a digital emotional diary for capturing one meaningful thought every day. The app featured Nini’s sketches and Levan’s clean algorithms. At the launch presentation, Levan told the audience: “This isn’t just code; it’s code written with love.” Today, they are collaborating on their next chapter, aptly titled “We”.
